What is Creator Booth?

Creator Booth is an AI assistant built only for football. Ask it to break down a match, scout a player, dig up the latest news, turn your research into a match-day script for your next video, or trade banter about your club.

The problem

General chatbots try to do everything. In football that often means out-of-date answers: last season's manager, a squad that has changed, a result that never happened, all delivered with total confidence. And if you make football content, you still spend hours collecting facts from different sites before you can write a single line.

How it helps

Checks real data first. For today's fixtures and the clubs you mention, it looks up live match facts and the club's current coach before it answers. When it finds something, a green Verified badge lists exactly which facts were confirmed.
Shows its sources. When it searches the web, the sources are listed under the reply so you can open them and check.
Built for creators. Script Studio turns research into something you can read on camera. Pick Short for quick answers or Deep for a full breakdown.
Banter on request. Set your favourite club in your profile and ask for a joke; it teases your rivals. It only does this when you ask.
Honest about limits. It is told never to invent stats, and to say so when it doesn't know.
