1. What Creator Booth is

Creator Booth is a tool that helps content creators research and draft short-form video scripts, using AI models (including Google's Gemini and Groq's hosted models) and third-party data sources (including API-Football). Some responses are grounded in live web search or sports data; others may rely on the underlying AI model's training data.

2. AI-generated content isn't guaranteed accurate

  • Scripts, facts, stats, and summaries are generated or assisted by AI and may contain errors, omissions, or outdated information — even when a source link is shown.
  • You're responsible for verifying any factual claim (scores, quotes, transfer news, historical claims, etc.) before publishing content based on it.
  • When a response falls back to a backup AI model without live web search, Creator Booth will tell you — treat that content with extra caution.

3. Your account and content

  • If you sign in (via Google or email), your chats and messages are stored so you can return to them later.
  • If you use Creator Booth without signing in, your chats are stored only on your device (local storage) and are not backed up anywhere.
  • You can delete your account and all associated chats at any time from Settings → Profile → Delete Account. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

See our Privacy Policy for details on how this is used and stored. You can view, change, or clear this information at any time from Settings → Profile, and answering it is never required to use Creator Booth.
